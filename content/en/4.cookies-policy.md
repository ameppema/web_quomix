# Cookies Policy – Quomix SAC

**Last updated:** August 6, 2025

In compliance with **Law 34/2002** on Information Society Services and Electronic Commerce (LSSICE), the **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)**, and Peru's **Law No. 29733**, we inform users of the site [https://quomix.io](https://quomix.io) about the use of cookies.

## 1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites store in the user's browser to collect technical and statistical information about their browsing.

They are not harmful to your device and are essential for the operation of many digital services.

## 2. Types of cookies we use

### a) By the entity that manages them:

- **First-party**: managed by Quomix SAC.
- **Third-party**: managed by external services (e.g. Google Analytics).

### b) By how long they remain active:

- **Session**: deleted when the browser is closed.
- **Persistent**: stored for a defined period.

### c) By their purpose:

- **Technical**: necessary for the basic operation of the site.
- **Preference**: remember the user's settings.
- **Analytical**: allow browsing to be measured and analyzed.
- **Marketing**: track the user to display relevant advertising.

## 3. Cookies used

Example of cookies we might use:

| Cookie         | Type        | Purpose                                  | Duration    | Manager      |
|----------------|-------------|------------------------------------------|-------------|--------------|
| `__cf_bm`      | Technical   | Protection against bots and attacks      | 30 min      | Cloudflare   |
| `_ga`          | Analytical  | Distinguish users (Google Analytics)     | 2 years     | Google       |
| `_gid`         | Analytical  | Analyze sessions                         | 24 hours    | Google       |
| `cookie_notice`| Technical   | Store cookie preferences                 | 1 year      | Quomix SAC   |

*This table is for reference and may vary. A periodic audit is recommended.*

## 4. Consent

When accessing the site for the first time, a notice will be displayed where the user can:

- Accept all cookies.
- Reject them (except the necessary ones).
- Configure their preferences.

The user may modify their consent at any time from the website or by configuring their browser.

## 5. How to disable or delete cookies

The user can configure their browser to allow, block, or delete cookies. Below are links to instructions by browser:

- [Google Chrome](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647)
- [Mozilla Firefox](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/cookies-information-websites-store-on-your-computer)
- [Safari](https://support.apple.com/en-us/guide/safari/sfri11471/mac)
- [Microsoft Edge](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/help/4027947)
- [Opera](https://help.opera.com/en/latest/web-preferences/#cookies)

Disabling cookies may affect the operation of the site.

## 6. Cookies and personal data

The use of some cookies may involve the processing of personal data. This data will be processed in accordance with the provisions of our [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy).

## 7. Changes to the policy

This Cookies Policy may be updated. Regular review is recommended. Any substantial change will be notified to the user.
