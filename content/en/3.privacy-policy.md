# Privacy Policy – Quomix SAC

In compliance with **Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR)**, Spain's **Organic Law 3/2018**, and Peru's **Law No. 29733**, we inform users of the website [https://quomix.io](https://quomix.io) about how we process their personal data.

## 1. Identity of the data controller

**Controller**: Quomix SAC  
**Registered address**: Av. Iquitos 755, Dpto 202, Primero 5ta, Lima 15646, Peru  
**Contact email**: [contact@quomix.io](mailto:contact@quomix.io)  
**Activity**: Software development and technology consulting.

## 2. Personal data collected

We may collect the following categories of data:

- **Identification data**: first name, last name, company.
- **Contact data**: email, phone.
- **Technical data**: IP, browser, operating system, cookies.
- **Voluntary data**: any information the user submits.

We do not process sensitive data.

## 3. Purposes of processing

We process data to:

- Respond to inquiries or requests.
- Manage business relationships.
- Send information about services (with prior consent).
- Analyze navigation on a statistical basis.
- Comply with legal obligations.

## 4. Legal basis for processing

The legal bases are:

- **User consent**.
- **Performance of contracts or pre-contractual steps**.
- **Compliance with legal obligations**.
- **Legitimate interest** of Quomix SAC, always respecting the user's rights.

## 5. Data retention

Data will be retained:

- Inquiries: up to 1 year after the last communication.
- Clients: for the duration of the contractual relationship and legal periods.
- Communications: until consent is withdrawn.
- Technical data: in accordance with the cookies policy.

Once the periods have elapsed, data will be deleted or anonymized.

## 6. Recipients

Data may be shared with:

- Providers acting as **data processors**.
- Administrative or judicial authorities, if legally required.
- Technology platforms (Google, AWS, etc.), under appropriate safeguards.

Data is not sold or transferred to third parties for commercial purposes.

## 7. International transfers

If we use services outside the EU or Peru, we ensure:

- Countries with an adequate level of protection.
- EU standard contractual clauses.
- Express consent of the user.

## 8. User rights

The user may exercise the following rights:

- **Access**
- **Rectification**
- **Erasure**
- **Objection**
- **Restriction of processing**
- **Portability**
- **Withdrawal of consent**

Send an email to [contact@quomix.io](mailto:contact@quomix.io) stating your name, the right you are exercising, and a copy of your identity document.

## 9. Complaints

You may file a complaint with:

- **Spain**: Spanish Data Protection Agency – [www.aepd.es](https://www.aepd.es)  
- **Peru**: National Authority for the Protection of Personal Data – [www.gob.pe/anpd](https://www.gob.pe/anpd)

## 10. Minors

The site is not directed at children under 14. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If detected, it will be deleted.

## 11. Security measures

We apply appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure the security and integrity of personal data (encryption, access control, backups, etc.).

## 12. Changes to the policy

This policy may be updated. Periodic review is recommended. Any relevant changes will be notified to registered users.
